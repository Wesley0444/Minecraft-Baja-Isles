# Retired datapacks

Kept, not deleted — restore by moving the folder back up one level.

## parity-underreward
Retired 2026-08-30. Buffed the melee capstones of Twilight Forest, The Undergarden and
Deeper and Darker to "match the hours they cost".

**Why retired:** `planning/03-FINAL-DECISIONS.md §0.2` dropped the entire under-reward half
of the audit. Under-reward was a defect only while the pack was framed as *exclusive
parallel tracks*. In a kitchen-sink pack where everyone plays everything and gear is freely
giftable, an under-paying mod is just content you do for fun. Buffing it is scope the group
explicitly declined.

Restore only if the pack is ever re-framed around strict parity.
